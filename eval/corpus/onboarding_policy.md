# Engineering onboarding policy

## Access

New engineers receive repository access on their first day. Production database access requires
completion of the data handling course and is granted no earlier than **day 30**.

All access requests are approved by the engineer's manager and reviewed quarterly.

## The first ninety days

- **Days 1 to 14** — pairing with an assigned buddy. No solo production changes.
- **Days 15 to 45** — solo changes permitted behind a feature flag, reviewed by two engineers.
- **Days 46 to 90** — standard review process, one approver.

An engineer is considered fully onboarded after their **fifth** merged pull request, or at day
90, whichever comes later.

## Equipment

Every engineer receives a laptop, an external monitor and a budget of **£400** for a desk setup.
The budget resets annually on 1 April.

## Escalation

Anything blocking an engineer for more than **four hours** should be escalated to the team lead.
The onboarding buddy is expected to respond within one working day.

## Exceptions

Contractors follow the same access policy but do not receive the equipment budget. Interns are
not granted production database access at any point.
